What the numbers mean

Mean
The average: the sum divided by the count. Sensitive to extreme values.
Median
The middle value when sorted. Barely affected by extremes.
Mode
The most frequent value. A set can have several modes, or none.
Std deviation
How spread out values are around the mean (sample version, n − 1).
Q1 / Q3 / IQR
The 25th and 75th percentiles and the distance between them.
Skewness
Above 0 means a long tail of high values; below 0 means a tail of low ones.
