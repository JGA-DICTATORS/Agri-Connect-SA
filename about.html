About AgriConnect SA

Connecting South African farmers with buyers through technology.

Who We Are

AgriConnect SA is a digital agricultural marketplace developed to bridge the gap between farmers and consumers. The platform enables farmers to advertise and sell fresh produce directly to buyers, reducing reliance on intermediaries while improving market access.

The system was created as a web-based solution that demonstrates how technology can improve efficiency within the agricultural sector by providing a secure, simple and accessible marketplace.

Our Mission

To empower South African farmers by providing an online marketplace where they can reach customers nationwide while offering buyers affordable, fresh and quality agricultural products.

Our Vision

To become South Africa's leading digital agricultural marketplace that promotes sustainable farming, food security and economic growth through innovation.

How AgriConnect Works

  1. Farmers create an account.
  2. Farmers list their agricultural products.
  3. Buyers browse the online marketplace.
  4. Products are added to the shopping cart.
  5. Customers complete checkout securely.
  6. Farmers receive the order.
  7. Products are delivered to customers.

Why Choose AgriConnect?

  • Fresh products directly from farmers.
  • Secure online shopping experience.
  • Affordable pricing.
  • Nationwide accessibility.
  • Simple and user-friendly interface.
  • Supports local farming communities.
